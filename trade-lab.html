How the Lab values players

Baseline = average of available player scores in the last five finalized league weeks. Scores already reflect this league’s scoring rules. Zero scores are included, including byes where supplied; missing samples are unknown and treated as zero until you enter an estimate. These are recent scoring baselines, not forecasts, rest-of-season rankings, injury adjustments, or market prices.

Edit a player’s baseline in either roster. Estimates stay in this tab and reset on refresh. The optimizer assigns each player once to a legal starting slot, including Superflex. IR and taxi players do not start; incoming players enter the active roster. Injury flags are displayed, not automatically excluded. Drops required by package trades must be resolved separately.

Win Now ranking = 2 × your lineup gain + 0.35 × partner gain − (100 − value balance) / 20. Mutual Benefit uses equal weights for lineup gains. Value balance compares the sums of nonnegative player baselines. No acceptance probability is implied. Draft picks, FAAB, waivers, keeper contracts and automatic trade submission are outside this player-only analyzer.
